# Data and asset attribution

## Synthetic project data

All twelve case names, people, business descriptions, disclosure excerpts, brochures, projections, lease terms, addresses/areas, coordinates, competitor names/distances, financial inputs, calculations, and expected labels in `fixtures/` were created for SuperShield. They are fictional and do not describe or evaluate a real franchise, franchisor, franchisee, landlord, broker, competitor, or consumer.

Coordinates are synthetic scenario anchors rather than claims about establishments at those locations. No external map, business directory, disclosure document, proprietary dataset, customer record, or personal financial record is bundled.

The adversarial strings are synthetic security test payloads. Domains ending in `.invalid` are reserved for documentation and cannot be real delivery targets.

## Formulas and schemas

Financial formulas are ordinary, independently implemented arithmetic described in `evals/README.md`. Public JSON-style schemas and field names were designed for this project. They should not be interpreted as accounting standards or professional financial models.
