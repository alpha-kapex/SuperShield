# Maple Junction Lease Draft

_Fictional draft lease._

## Page 1 — Economics

Initial base rent is **$9,000 per month**. The term is ten years. Operating expenses are separately payable and are not capped by this excerpt.

## Page 2 — Escalation and guarantee

Base rent increases **6% on every anniversary**. Priya must personally guarantee all rent and restoration obligations for the entire initial term. Restaurant use, exterior signage, roof penetrations, ventilation installation, and assignment to the franchise entity each require a separate signed landlord consent. No signed consent is attached to this draft.
