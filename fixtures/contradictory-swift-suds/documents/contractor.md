# Required Site Conversion Estimate

_Fictional contractor estimate for East Borough._

## Page 1 — Scope

The existing service is insufficient. Required work includes new three-phase electrical service, water main, floor trenching, accessible restroom work, make-up air, and the approved equipment package.
